=== Description (max 4000) ===
Mini Fighter is a fast, colorful fighting game in the style of the classic beat-em-ups, with more than 20 fighters, 14 arenas and special moves for every character. Play solo, against the computer, or online with friends, all for free!

ONLINE BATTLES WITH FRIENDS
Open a room, share the code and fight together, up to 8 players from anywhere. Free-for-all, duel, or color teams: blue, red, green and purple. Any split you like, plus computer fighters on your side.

ROAD TO THE TOP
13 stages, a different boss in each one and a final boss at the end. Progress is saved after every stage.

20+ FIGHTERS
Every fighter has their own powers: fire, ice, lightning, wind, rock and more. And at the end, the Shadow and the Virus are waiting for you.

SPECIAL MOVES
At least 4 special attacks per fighter, combos, runs, dashes, grabs and throws. A single swipe on the move wheel fires a special.

MADE FOR IPHONE
Comfortable joystick and buttons, fitted to the full iPhone screen.

The game is free, contains ads and offers optional in-app purchases (gems, fighters, skins and a season pass), each protected by a parent gate. Reward chests are earned by playing or bought with in-game currency only; their odds are shown in the game and at minifighter.co.il/odds

Website: https://minifighter.co.il

=== App Review notes (paste into "Notes" in App Review Information) ===
No login is needed. Progress is saved on the device and, automatically, in our cloud (an anonymous account with a 10-character restore code in Profile; no personal details).
ONLINE: Lobby > the big fight button. Ranked modes (duel, duos, crown, free-for-all) fill empty seats with computer fighters after a few seconds, so one device can test a full match. The classic friend rooms are still in Main menu > מולטיפלייר.
FRIENDS: Lobby > friends button (under the party button). Each player has a public player tag (Profile, e.g. #K7QM2X9A); add a friend by tag, accept on the second device, then invite to a party (up to 3). There is NO free chat anywhere: players only see nicknames (16 characters) and can send 5 predefined emotes.
IN-APP PURCHASES (30 products, see each product's review note): Lobby > "חנות" (Shop) and Lobby > "כרטיס עונה" (Season Pass). Every real-money purchase first shows a PARENT GATE ("אישור הורים"): a multiplication written in Hebrew words, e.g. "כמה זה שבע כפול שמונה?" (seven times eight), answered with digits (56). One correct answer allows one purchase; three wrong answers lock it for a minute. Restore: Shop > "שחזור רכישות".
To test purchases use a Sandbox Apple Account (Settings > App Store > Sandbox Account). Gem packs are consumable; fighters, skins, bundles and the starter offer are non-consumable; the season pass is a non-renewing subscription for the current 28-day season.
RANDOM ITEMS: reward chests are earned by playing or bought only with in-game coins or gems (never directly for money); the odds are shown in the game before buying and at https://minifighter.co.il/odds/
The old "Full Version" (premium_all) is no longer sold in 2.0; owners keep it and Shop > "שחזור רכישות" restores it.
ADS: Google AdMob (banner on menus, interstitials between matches, optional rewarded ads). Never during a fight. The App Tracking Transparency prompt appears before any ad loads.
The interface is in Hebrew.
